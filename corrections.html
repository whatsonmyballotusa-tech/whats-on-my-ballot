Report a correction

We will get things wrong sometimes. What matters is what happens next. Fill out the form — your report is saved on this device and then opened in your email app, prefilled, for you to send. Factual errors are fixed within 48 hours — within 24 hours in the two weeks before an election.

No account needed. Nothing is uploaded — the report opens in your email app and you send it yourself.

Candidates: claim your profile

If you're a candidate (or on a campaign team):

  1. Use the form above with "You are" set to candidate or campaign staff, and email us from your official campaign or government address so we can verify it's you.
  2. Tell us what's missing or wrong — bio, photo, contact info, platform quotes — with sources.
  3. We review against our verification rules and publish with a changelog note: "Updated [date] at the candidate's request."

Claiming a profile lets you correct facts. It does not let you rewrite your profile, remove sourced facts, or change how opponents are presented. The identical-layout rule applies to everyone.
