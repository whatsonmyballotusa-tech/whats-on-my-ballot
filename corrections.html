Corrections policy

We will get things wrong sometimes. What matters is what happens next.

Report an error

Email whatsonmyballotusa@gmail.com with the profile link, what's wrong, and a source we can check. Factual errors are fixed within 48 hours — within 24 hours in the two weeks before an election.

Candidates: claim your profile

If you're a candidate (or on a campaign team):

  1. Email us from your official campaign or government address so we can verify it's you.
  2. Tell us what's missing or wrong — bio, photo, contact info, platform quotes.
  3. We review, verify against public sources, and publish with a changelog note: "Updated [date] at the candidate's request."

Claiming a profile lets you correct facts. It does not let you rewrite your profile, remove sourced facts, or change how opponents are presented. The identical-layout rule applies to everyone.
