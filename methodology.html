Methodology: how we stay non-partisan

Trust is the product. These rules are enforced on every profile we publish — no exceptions, no editorial judgment calls.

1. Identical profiles for every candidate

Every candidate card has the same sections in the same order: photo, party, office sought, bio, contact, key votes, platform, sources. If a section has no verified information for one candidate, it shows "unverified" — it is never quietly omitted for one candidate and filled in for another.

2. Platforms are quoted, not paraphrased

"Running on" sections quote the candidate's own published materials, labeled as such. We don't summarize, interpret, or characterize positions.

3. Every fact carries a source

Bios, votes, and contact details must cite at least one public source, shown to you under "Sources." Fields are scored by confidence:

  • verified — confirmed by official (.gov) or multiple independent sources
  • unverified — single or unconfirmed source, or not yet reviewed

Anything we can't source is labeled unverified — we never fill gaps with guesses.

4. No color-coding, no ranking, no endorsements

Parties are shown as plain text labels in a neutral style. We don't order candidates to favor anyone (ballot order follows the official ballot), and we never accept money from candidates, parties, or PACs.

5. Corrections are public

Mistakes get fixed and logged. Candidates can claim their profile to request corrections — see our corrections policy.

6. Unofficial by definition

We compile public data; we are not the government. Every ballot view carries this notice: "Unofficial voter information compiled from public sources. Not affiliated with any government. Verify your ballot, polling place, and deadlines with your state or local election office."
