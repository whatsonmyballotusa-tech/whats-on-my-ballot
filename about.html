About

What's On My Ballot is a free, non-partisan voter-information project. Type in your address and see exactly what's on your ballot — every race, every candidate — with bios, photos, contact info, voting records, and platforms.

Why it exists

Most voters can name the top of the ticket and almost nothing below it. Judicial retentions, state house races, ballot questions — these shape daily life far more than most people realize, and reliable information about them is scattered across dozens of sites. We put it in one place.

Non-partisan by construction

We don't endorse candidates, rank them, or tell you how to vote. Every candidate gets the identical profile layout, platforms are quoted in the candidates' own words, and every fact carries a source. Read the full rules in our methodology.

Philadelphia pilot

We're starting with Philadelphia for the November 3, 2026 general election, then expanding — one well-covered area at a time, because data quality beats coverage.

Who runs this

A small independent civic-tech team based in Philadelphia. We accept no money from candidates, parties, or PACs — see contact for how to reach us.
