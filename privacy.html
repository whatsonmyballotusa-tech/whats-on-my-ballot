Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026. Short version: we don't want your data.

Addresses and ZIP codes

Ballot lookups run on your device. We do not store, log, or transmit your street address or ZIP code to our servers. When you enter a full address, it is sent only to the geocoding service needed to resolve your district, and only at the moment you ask.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate usage — e.g., how many ballots were viewed. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal profiles.

Email list (optional)

If you sign up for election reminders, we store only your email address and use it solely for the reminders you requested. Unsubscribe anytime with one click; we never sell or share email addresses.

Advertising

If we run ads in the future, they appear only in clearly labeled "Advertisement" blocks. We do not build advertising profiles or share personal data with ad networks.

Contact

Questions about this policy: whatsonmyballotusa@gmail.com.
