What to send where

  • Corrections — wrong fact on a profile? See our corrections policy first, then email us with the profile link and a source.
  • Candidates & campaigns — want to claim your profile or supply missing info (bio, photo, platform)? Email from your official campaign or government address.
  • Press — we're happy to talk about non-partisan voter information.
  • Sponsorships — we accept civic sponsors (universities, libraries, nonprofits). We do not accept money from candidates, parties, or PACs.

We aim to reply within two business days — faster in the two weeks before an election.
