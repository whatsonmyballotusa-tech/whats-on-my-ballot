Why we ask for your address

Best

Full street address

Pinpoints your exact voting district and shows you precisely your ballot. Nothing stored, ever.

Good

ZIP+4

More precise than a 5-digit ZIP — but exact lookup still needs an API key we don't have yet, so you'll confirm your districts manually.

Fallback

5-digit ZIP

One ZIP can span several districts, so we'll ask you to confirm your district before showing a ballot.

How we stay non-partisan

Every candidate gets the identical profile layout. Platforms are quoted in the candidates' own words. Every fact carries a source — and anything unverified is labeled unverified, never guessed. Read our methodology.
